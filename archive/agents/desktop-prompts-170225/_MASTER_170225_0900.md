## 🎨 DESIGN-PRINZIPIEN (Basierend auf bestehendem Dashboard)

### **1. Glassmorphism & Liquid Glass**
- ✅ Backdrop Blur: `backdrop-blur-xl`, `backdrop-blur-sm`
- ✅ Semi-transparent Backgrounds: `bg-white/5`, `bg-black/30`
- ✅ Subtle Borders: `border-white/10`
- ✅ Rounded Corners: `rounded-2xl`

### **2. Gradient Background**
```css
bg-gradient-to-br from-gray-900 via-blue-900 to-gray-900
```

### **3. Typography & Colors**
- Headers: `text-white`, `font-bold`
- Subtext: `text-white/70`, `text-white/60`, `text-white/50`
- Interactive Elements: Hover → `text-white`

### **4. Spacing & Layout**
- Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Padding: `p-6`, `py-8`
- Gap: `gap-2`, `gap-4`

### **5. Icons**
- Emoji Icons: `🎮`, `💡`, `✓`
- Lucide Icons: `ArrowLeft`, etc.
- Size: `text-3xl`, `text-4xl`
